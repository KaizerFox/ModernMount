# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make the ModernMount community welcoming, safe, and equitable for
everyone who participates in good faith.

We respect the dignity, rights, experiences, and contributions of all people,
regardless of identity, background, ability, language, belief, education, or
social and economic position.

## Encouraged behavior

- Engage kindly, honestly, and constructively.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept useful feedback with care.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair harm.
- Credit sources and respect confidentiality.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or unwanted personal attention.
- Insults, character attacks, discrimination, or demeaning comments.
- Sexualized conduct or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Impersonation or evasion of moderation actions.
- Deliberate disruption, unsafe technical advice, or promotion outside normal
  community expectations.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the
project in public, including official accounts, events, and communications.

## Reporting and enforcement

Once the GitHub repository is connected, sensitive reports should use GitHub's
private reporting tools. Do not put sensitive reports in a public issue.

Project moderators will investigate reports privately, protect confidentiality
as far as practical, and may issue a warning, temporarily limit participation,
suspend access, or permanently ban someone depending on severity and pattern.
Moderators who have a conflict of interest must step aside.
