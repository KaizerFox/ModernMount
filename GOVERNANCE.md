# Project governance

ModernMount is currently a founder-led project. The project owner acts as lead
maintainer and has final responsibility for product direction, releases,
security response, licensing, and accepting contributions.

## Decision making

Routine decisions are made through public issues and pull-request review when
possible. Storage safety, privilege boundaries, supported platforms, and public
license changes require explicit approval from the project owner. The owner may
decline changes that increase data-loss risk, support burden, or maintenance
cost even when they are technically sound.

## Maintainers

Additional maintainers may be appointed for specific areas. Maintainer access
does not by itself grant authority to change licensing, security disclosure
policy, or project ownership.

## Contributions

Contributors retain copyright in their work and submit it under the project's
GPL-3.0-or-later terms. Acceptance of a contribution does not create employment,
partnership, or a promise of compensation.

## Changes to governance

The project owner may update this document publicly. Changes do not revoke
rights already granted under an existing license or signed agreement.
